# Catering and staffing

Fictional planning agreement: Shah Halal will provide 200 meals at $24 per guest, for $4,800. A venue change requires one consolidated confirmation covering delivery address, date and time, unloading access, setup and dietary requirements. Do not authorize charges or a new booking in that message.

Four staff are budgeted at $300 each. The scenario staffing policy is one staff member per 60 guests, rounded up. Assign an arrival host, catering receiver, presentation lead and event lead; names will be assigned later.
