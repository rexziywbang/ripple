# Role cards and handoffs

Owner: Event lead · Working plan

## Roles

**Event lead:** owns the running order, final supplier decisions and program cues. Keeps the contact sheet and current run of show available offline. Decides when to delay an award or presentation if dinner service is still underway.

**Arrival host:** checks the entrance and wayfinding, greets guests and directs coats and seating. Knows the accessible route. Once arrivals settle, joins room support after explicitly handing late arrivals to the event lead.

**Service liaison:** receives catering, checks the delivery against the order and confirms where dietary meals will be held. Coordinates the service pause before awards and reports missing items without announcing them to the room.

**Production support:** checks microphone, display, laptop connection and slides before guests arrive. Keeps an offline slide copy and the presenter's notes. Gives the event lead a clear ready signal.

## Scaling the team

The scenario's staffing guideline is one event staff member per 60 guests at $300 each. This is an internal rehearsal assumption, not a universal staffing rule. Role conflicts matter as well as the ratio.

With one person staffing a small dinner, arrange catering delivery before guest arrival and complete the AV check first. If those activities overlap, propose a revised schedule or extra help rather than assigning the same person to multiple places.

## Handoff checklist

- Before doors: entrance checked, service arrival known, dietary meals accounted for, AV tested.
- Before dinner: guests seated, supplier ready, water available, late-arrival route clear.
- Before awards: clearing paused, microphone ready, presenter cued, lights appropriate.
- Before close: guest belongings, leftover-food arrangements, equipment packing and venue handback covered.

Use roles until the organizer supplies real assignments. Do not invent staff names or imply someone has accepted a shift.
