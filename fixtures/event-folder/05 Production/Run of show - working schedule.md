## Adaptation notes

Attendance changes the service duration. A ten-person dinner may not need a formal arrival desk or a forty-five-minute meal-service window. A larger event may need several service points and a longer buffer. Keep the event's social rhythm when revising the schedule.

If catering is late, extend conversation and move the welcome slightly. Avoid starting an amplified presentation while suppliers cross the room with hot food. If AV is unavailable, deliver the short presentation from printed notes and preserve the awards.

The approved operating cards are the current run of show when they differ from this initial draft. Send one consolidated staff brief after the event lead reviews the changes.
