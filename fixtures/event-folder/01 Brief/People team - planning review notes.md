# People team · Planning review

September 18, 2026 · Working notes  
Participants: People Operations, Workplace, Finance, Internal Communications

## What we agreed

Dinner stays central. Keep the presentation to ten minutes and the awards to fifteen unless the event lead approves a different running order. No keynote, sponsor segment or elaborate stage build is needed.

The team wants something more considered than a generic corporate invitation. The preferred direction is a warm evening illustration with deep plum, candlelight, small festive details and generous empty space. Avoid the look of a sales announcement.

Attendees should receive one useful update when an arrangement changes, not a stream of internal planning activity. Supplier messages should contain the current date, headcount, venue and the precise answer needed. The organizer should be able to edit the actual email before approving it.

## Practical concerns

- Workplace: a room that fits on paper may still be awkward once the buffet, projector and arrival area are placed. Ask about the room layout rather than relying on the entire building's capacity.
- People Operations: the latest dietary notes include kosher options. Halal catering alone does not resolve this. A separate meal order may be easier than replacing the entire menu.
- Production: a venue advertising AV may only provide a screen or television. Confirm the microphone, audio output and the presenter's laptop connection before releasing the rental.
- Finance: retain deposits and cancellation fees when comparing replacements. The remaining budget is not the same as cash available to place a second order.

## Follow-ups

Workplace will compare published room information with the current event format. People Operations will obtain a menu and service plan. Production will identify the minimum kit needed for the final program. Internal Communications will prepare the invitation design once the intended tone is clear.

These notes record the discussion, not supplier confirmation. Later reviewed decisions supersede them.
