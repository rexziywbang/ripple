# Room, access and service requirements

Owner: Workplace · September planning brief

## Room fit

The space needs to suit the current guest count and event format. For a seated meal, use a published banquet or seated-dinner figure for the specific room. Allow practical space for food service and the short presentation; do not substitute theater or standing capacity.

For a group below 20, consider whether a private dining room or a single long table gives a better experience than a ballroom. The room choice should be reviewed before incurring a new minimum spend or cancellation charge.

## Access questions

- When can the organizer and supplier enter? Is that time included in the rental?
- Which entrance is step-free, and how does a guest reach the room from it?
- Is there a separate loading point? What is the path from delivery to the service area?
- Is there a lift, a cart, a loading time restriction or a staffed receiving desk?
- What are the room-close and load-out deadlines?

The guest invitation needs the guest entrance. The catering brief needs the delivery entrance. These may be different even when the street address is the same.

## Food and equipment constraints

Check whether outside catering is allowed, restricted to preferred suppliers or subject to a fee. Confirm access to a staging area, food-safe storage, power and waste disposal. A venue's preferred caterer is not automatically suitable for every dietary requirement.

House AV should be itemized: display, audio system, microphone, connections, podium and technical support. Determine whether those items are included, separately priced or merely available to hire. Keep the existing equipment estimate until equivalent scope is established and any cancellation has been reviewed.

## Garden Hall baseline

The original scenario uses a fictional Garden Hall agreement. It is useful for rehearsal comparisons but cannot prove availability, a real address or current room specifications. Later published venue research and the current event plan take precedence.
