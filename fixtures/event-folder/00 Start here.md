# Northstar · Christmas dinner

Working folder for the People team, Workplace, Finance and event suppliers.

The PRD explains the experience we want. The other folders carry the practical constraints: room access, food service, presentation equipment, staff handoffs and guest communications. Ripple keeps the current event plan and reviewed operating plans beside these background documents.

| Folder | Contents | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 Brief | Event PRD, stakeholder meeting notes | People Operations |
| 02 Venue | Room and access requirements | Workplace |
| 03 Catering | Service brief and supplier correspondence | People Operations |
| 04 Staff | Role cards and handoff checklist | Event lead |
| 05 Production | Run of show and AV requirements | Production lead |
| 06 Finance | Budget assumptions and purchasing rules | Finance |
| 07 Invitations | Creative brief and invitation artwork | Internal Communications |

Background documents describe the September planning baseline. The current Ripple event plan records later changes to the guest count, venue, vendors, staffing and budget. A proposed schedule is not an approved commitment; supplier quotes and venue availability need their own confirmation.

## Scenario provenance

This folder is a fictional Northstar planning scenario prepared for the Ripple demonstration. Staff roles, supplier correspondence, agreements, addresses and financial amounts in the background documents are invented. Reserved `.example` addresses do not receive email. These materials are not evidence of a real booking or transaction. Published venue research, if added later, keeps its own source links and status.
