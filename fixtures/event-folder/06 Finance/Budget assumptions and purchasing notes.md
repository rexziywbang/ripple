## Accounting treatment

The $600 catering deposit is included in the original catering amount. If the supplier confirms cancellation and retains the deposit, move that retained cost into the cancellation total without counting it twice. Release only the outstanding commitment that the supplier confirms is no longer payable.

An awaited quote is unknown. Do not replace it with a zero-cost service or a guessed per-person price. A carried estimate may remain useful, but the organizer should be able to distinguish it from a quote for the new supplier or room.

Equipment and staff rates here are scenario assumptions. Prefer actual supplier scope and quotes when available. A staffing count is a planning estimate until people are assigned; a lower guest count does not prove that every booked staff shift can be canceled without cost.

## Comparison format

When proposing a change, show the old commitment, the proposed replacement, retained costs and the net forecast. Include delivery, taxes, service fees and required extras when supplied. Highlight any missing price component instead of burying it in a total.

No purchase, deposit or booking is authorized by this document. Commitments follow the organizer's reviewed action.
