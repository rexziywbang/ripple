## Questions the public record did not resolve

- Full legal and brand roster of acquired businesses and acquisition prices.
- Current capitalization, ownership percentages, and independently confirmed valuation.
- Consolidated audited earnings and acquisition-adjusted organic growth.
- Deployment coverage and per-workflow accuracy, review burden, inference and integration costs.
- Customer-data rights, cross-company data boundaries, and security-control details.
- Independently measured employee retention, headcount effects, customer retention, and service quality.
- Closing of the proposed Amex GBT transaction after the August 4 update.

Do not substitute the Houston homebuilder Long Lake Ltd., unrelated asset managers, or similarly named organizations for this entity. Pipeline is not realized revenue; acquired profit is not AI-generated profit; faster agent setup is not completed business transformation.
